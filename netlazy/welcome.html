01 // HOW IT WORKS

how it works

One-click registration

You can create an account in one click—without entering an email or phone number.

Customize your profile

Next, fill out the form, select any tags, attach a photo, video, or audio, tell us about yourself, and provide your contact information.

Public and private contacts

Contacts can be public or private. Public contacts are displayed in your profile, while private contacts will only be visible to the specific user, with your consent.

Three ways to request contacts

Next, you can begin choosing a profile. You can send a request for private contacts in three ways:
• Share - share a contact unilaterally
• Exchange - exchange contacts simultaneously
• Demand - ask the user for their contact information without sharing yours
