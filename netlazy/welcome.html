02 // CORE FEATURES

what we have

profiles feed instead of swipes

You get a feed of profiles so you can find partners more effectively.

scrollable chronological bulletin stream

lots and lots of tags

We try to add tags to practically everything, so you can find profiles more efficiently and stay in control of your search.

three ways to like a profile

Unlike standard likes, our requests revolve around managing each other's contact details. You can: share, exchange, or demand them. (And leaving a request unanswered serves as a native blacklist.)

and a little bit of cryptography

Registration works in just one click, your account is not tied to any email or phone number, and your password never leaves your device.
